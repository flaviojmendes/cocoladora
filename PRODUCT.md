# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Office workers, remote professionals, and everyday humor enthusiasts looking to calculate their paid earnings during restroom breaks, track their bathroom "paycheck" history, rate restroom facilities, and leave quirky graffiti messages on a virtual toilet door.

## Product Purpose
A viral, humorous, and delightfully crafted utility web application ("Cocoladora"). It allows users to calculate how much money they earned while meditating on the throne, rate restrooms and view them on an interactive map, and write graffiti on a virtual toilet door. Deployed seamlessly on Vercel with Vercel Serverless Functions and persistent Vercel Postgres storage.

## Brand Personality
Playful, irreverent, tongue-in-cheek yet crisp, responsive, and high-craft. Warm earth tones, bold comic-inspired typographic character, and smooth tactile micro-interactions.

## Anti-references
- Fake/mock seed data injected into user spaces
- Dull corporate enterprise forms and generic dashboards
- AI slop tropes: generic purple/blue gradients, floating blobs, faux glassmorphism, washed-out low-contrast gray text
- Broken network requests, stale loaders, and unhandled database errors
- Clunky navigation and non-responsive layout bugs

## Design Principles
1. **Real Data on Vercel & Postgres**: Real serverless API endpoints backed by Vercel Postgres with zero fake data.
2. **Humor with High Craft**: The subject matter is funny, but the design is production-grade, accessible, and responsive across all screens.
3. **Tactile Polish**: Distinct interactive states (default, hover, active, focus), smooth 150-250ms transitions, and delightful details.
4. **Resilient Local Cache**: Gracefully caches data to maintain responsive rendering and offline tolerance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast compliant with WCAG AA.
- Clear focus outlines, semantic markup, and full keyboard navigation.
- Smooth transitions with support for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
